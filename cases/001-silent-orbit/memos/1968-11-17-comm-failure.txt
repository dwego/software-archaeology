ASTRA RESEARCH DIRECTORATE
FLIGHT TEST OPERATIONS

MEMORANDUM: FTO-68-1117
DATE: 17 NOVEMBER 1968

TO: ORPHEUS-3 SYSTEMS TEAM
FROM: FLIGHT TEST OPERATIONS

SUBJECT: COMMUNICATION FAILURE DURING GROUND TEST 042

Ground Test 042 was terminated following simultaneous telemetry
decoding failures and loss of radio channel acquisition.

The previous communication test completed without reported faults.

No hardware modifications were recorded between Ground Test 041
and Ground Test 042.

Flight Software reports that internal telemetry loopback testing
continues to pass.

Ground Systems continues to reject frames produced by the flight
computer.

The primary and backup radio channels also failed to acquire stable
ground lock.

Communication firmware build 68.11.14 was installed between the
two test sessions.

Further flight-software changes are suspended pending investigation.

-- E. Hollis
Flight Test Operations

P.S. Mission Operations would appreciate not discovering new
protocols fifteen minutes into a night test.
