ASTRA RESEARCH DIRECTORATE
GROUND SYSTEMS DIVISION

MEMORANDUM: GSD-68-1021
DATE: 21 OCTOBER 1968

TO: ORPHEUS FLIGHT SOFTWARE GROUP
FROM: GROUND SYSTEMS DIVISION

SUBJECT: GDU-4 REPLACEMENT SCHEDULE

The replacement Ground Decoder Unit will not be available before
the current ORPHEUS-3 launch window.

Flight software must therefore remain compatible with the existing
GDU-4 decoder until further notice.

ARD-COMM-12 Revision B remains the approved operational
communication specification for ORPHEUS-3.

Revision C material currently under evaluation may be used for
simulation and laboratory testing only.

Any flight implementation based on Revision C requires explicit
Systems approval and corresponding updates to ground equipment.

Do not deploy Revision C framing changes to ORPHEUS-3 flight
hardware.

-- M. Mercer
Ground Systems Division

P.S. If Flight Software changes the packet layout again before the
new decoder arrives, they can explain it to Mission Operations.
